# 🧠 Circadian & Cognitive Digital Twin (Proof-of-Concept)

> **Happiest Health Hackathon Submission**  
> A predictive "What-If" physiological simulator modeling the impact of sleep debt, caffeine timing, and screen exposure on afternoon cognitive clarity.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
Most health tools act as static logs—they summarize past events without predicting future physiological impacts. 

This **Digital Twin Proof-of-Concept** bridges tracking and actionability by creating a dynamic behavioral feedback loop. Users execute **"What-If" scenario simulations** to optimize caffeine intake, sleep windows, and focus curves in real time.

---

## ✨ Features
- **Dynamic "What-If" Simulator:** Real-time sliders alter caffeine timing, sleep quality, and screen habits to preview energy curves.
- **24-Hour Energy Curve:** Visualizes circadian alertness, sleep debt penalties, and caffeine half-life decay.
- **Deep Sleep Inhibition Index:** Calculates projected sleep disruption caused by late-day stimulant intake.
- **Actionable Optimization Engine:** Automatically generates recommendations based on modeled state shifts.

---

- **Frontend/UI:** Streamlit
- **Data & Plotting:** NumPy, Pandas, Plotly
